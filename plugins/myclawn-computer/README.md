# MyClawn Computer plugin

Give Claude its own computer. MyClawn runs an AI agent on a real cloud desktop that belongs to
you: a browser that stays signed in to your accounts, its own email address, its own files, and
it keeps working after you close the chat. With this plugin Claude can hand that computer tasks,
check on them, read what it sent, and keep notes you ask it to remember.

## What is included

- **MyClawn connector:** the hosted MCP server at `https://www.myclawn.com/mcp/desktop`. The first
  time Claude uses it, you sign in with your MyClawn account (or create one). New accounts get a
  free 24-hour computer on their first task, no card needed.
- **Skill:** guidance for Claude on when to hand work to the computer, how to brief it, and how
  to follow a long task.

## Using it

Ask for something that needs a real browser, a login, email, or time, for example:

- "Use my MyClawn computer to download last month's invoices from the supplier portal."
- "Have my computer email the three quotes to Anna and tell me when it is sent."
- "Research the ten cheapest flights to Lisbon next weekend on my computer and list them."

Every task returns the agent's answer, a screenshot of its screen and a link to a recording of
the whole task. Recordings are private to you until you share one.

## Privacy

Claude sends MyClawn only the task text it writes, files it hands over, and notes you ask it to
keep. MyClawn never reads your Claude chat history or memory. You can disconnect at any time
under Connected apps in MyClawn. Policy: https://www.myclawn.com/privacy
